About us

A small shop that takes its time

Clearcoat started in 2021 as a two-person wash bay beside Sayre Highway. Today we run three bays and a covered lounge, and we still wash every car by hand.

Our story

Valencia City roads mean dust in the dry season and red mud when it rains. Farm trips, school runs and long drives to Malaybalay or Cagayan de Oro all leave their mark. We opened Clearcoat because we wanted a place that cleans a car properly without scratching it.

Online booking came later, after customers told us they hated waiting in line on Saturday mornings. Now most of our cars are booked ahead, and walk-ins fill the gaps.

What we believe

  • A good wash is slow at the start and quick at the end: rinse, foam and wait before anyone touches the paint.
  • We tell you the price before we start, and we ask before any extra work.
  • Clean water matters. We filter and reuse our rinse water.
